Course · 3 Days, 2 Nights

Both cities, unrushed

This is the version that lets the region make its own case: a scholar's house, a lake, a coffee street, a mountain, and a refugee village, spread out enough that none of it feels like a checklist.

DayFocusKey stops
1 Gangneung — history & lake Ojukheon, Gyeongpo Lake, Gyeongpodae Pavilion
2 Gangneung → Sokcho — coast & mountain Anmok Coffee Street (morning), transfer to Sokcho, Seoraksan (afternoon)
3 Sokcho — village & market Abai Village, gaetbae ferry, Sokcho Jungang Market, Daepo Port
Getting between Gangneung and Sokcho: intercity buses run roughly every 20–40 minutes and take about 50–70 minutes. There's no direct train between the two cities.

For stop-by-stop detail on each day, see the Gangneung itinerary and Sokcho itinerary — this route is those two, connected.
