Course · Day Trip

Gangneung in one day, from Seoul

The KTX-Gangneung line (opened for the 2018 Winter Olympics) makes this genuinely doable without a night away — you're looking at roughly two hours each way from Seoul Station or Cheongnyangni. This route keeps history, coast, and coffee in one loop without feeling rushed.

Morning · KTX

Seoul → Gangneung

Take an early KTX from Seoul Station or Cheongnyangni Station. The ride itself is worth the window seat once the train clears the mountains near Pyeongchang.

Late morning

Ojukheon

A 15–20 minute taxi or bus from Gangneung Station. This is the birthplace of Yi I (Yulgok), one of Korea's two most influential Confucian scholars, and his mother Shin Saimdang, a painter and calligrapher whose portrait is on the 50,000-won note. Budget 45–60 minutes to walk the wooden house and grounds.

Midday

Lunch near Gyeongpo

Head toward Gyeongpo for a bowl of makguksu (buckwheat noodles) or fresh seafood. See the food guide for specific recommendations by neighborhood.

Early afternoon

Gyeongpo Lake & Beach

Rent a bike and ride the lake loop, or just walk the beach boardwalk. Gyeongpo is where locals actually go, not just tour buses.

Late afternoon

Anmok Coffee Street

Gangneung's café culture started here in the 1980s with a single roaster; now it's a full strip along the beach. Pick one place with an ocean-facing window rather than trying to hit five.

Evening · KTX

Gangneung → Seoul

Trains run until fairly late in the evening — check the current KTX timetable before you go, since schedules shift seasonally.

Doing this without a car: everything above is reachable by city bus or taxi from Gangneung Station, but taxis are the easier call if you're short on time — fares between these stops are modest.
