Course · 2 Days, 1 Night

Gangneung, properly

One night turns Gangneung from a photo stop into an actual visit. This route gives the scholar's house, the lake, the coffee street, and the fish market their own time slots instead of stacking all four into an afternoon.

Day 1

Morning

Arrive by KTX

Drop your bag at your accommodation if check-in allows, or store it at the station lockers.

Midday

Ojukheon & Gangneung Municipal Museum

The museum next door adds context you won't get from the house alone — regional history, not just the Yi family.

Afternoon

Gyeongpo Lake loop

Bike rental stalls circle the lake. The full loop takes about an hour at an easy pace, with pavilions to stop at along the way.

Evening

Jungang Market dinner

Gangneung's main traditional market — try dak-gangjeong (sweet-spicy fried chicken) or fresh seafood stalls.

Day 2

Morning

Anmok Beach & Coffee Street, unhurried

Go before 10am if you want a table by the window without a wait. Walk the beach afterward — this is where the espresso-and-ocean photo everyone posts actually comes from.

Late morning

Gyeongpodae Pavilion

A Joseon-era pavilion on a hill above the lake, built for viewing the moon reflected five times — in the sky, the sea, the lake, a wine cup, and a lover's eyes, according to the old saying.

Afternoon

KTX back to Seoul

Leave enough buffer to get back to the station — traffic near Gyeongpo can be heavier on weekends.
