What it was actually like

Itineraries tell you where to go. This page is for what happened when someone actually went — the detour that worked, the thing that didn't, the meal that was better than any guide promised. Two starter entries below; this is meant to fill up with real trips.

Solo traveler · 3 days · First visit to Korea outside Seoul
"I almost skipped Abai Village because it looked like just a food street in photos. The ferry crossing changed that in about ninety seconds — it's still how people who live there get around, not a ride."

Took the KTX from Seoul on a Friday morning, spent the first day in Gangneung around Ojukheon and the lake, then bused to Sokcho for Seoraksan the next day. The cable car line at Gwongeumseong was over an hour on a Saturday — worth going right when the park opens instead. Abai sundae from a restaurant two lanes back from the ferry landing was less crowded and, by a local's recommendation, better than the busiest spot on the main strip.

Traveling with a partner · 2 days · Coffee-focused trip
"We planned the whole trip around Anmok Coffee Street and ended up remembering Gyeongpodae Pavilion more."

Came specifically for the café scene and weren't disappointed — but the short walk up to Gyeongpodae Pavilion above the lake, mostly empty in the late afternoon, turned out to be the quiet highlight neither of us expected. Small addition to any coffee-street plan: it's a ten-minute walk from the lake loop and easy to add without reshuffling the day.
